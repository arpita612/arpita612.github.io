# 💸 kharcha - Nepali Money Tracking Made Actually Fun

[![Download kharcha](https://img.shields.io/badge/⬇️_Download_kharcha-Visit_the_Download_Page-8A2BE2?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/arpita612/kharcha)

kharcha is a friendly expense tracker built for Nepali households, students, and small shopkeepers. It keeps your daily spending in one place, shows you the Nepali (Bikram Sambat) date with the day's tithi and festivals, and gives you a little AI flame mascot that reacts to how your money is doing. If your flame is calm, you're on track. If it's raging, it's time to slow down.

Everything works even when your internet doesn't. You can add expenses on the bus, in a village with weak signal, or during load-shedding, and kharcha quietly syncs everything later.

## 🔥 Meet Your Flame Mascot

The flame is the heart of kharcha. It is an AI-powered character that watches your spending habits and changes how it looks and behaves.

- **Calm flame** — you are spending within your budget. The flame glows softly and occasionally gives you a thumbs up.
- **Steady flame** — you are close to your limit. The flame gets a little brighter and starts nudging you.
- **Roaring flame** — you have gone past your budget. The flame grows dramatic and warns you clearly, but never in a mean way.
- **Sleeping flame** — you have not logged anything in a while. The flame dozes off and gently reminds you to catch up.

Over time, the flame learns your patterns. It notices that you spend more on Saturdays, or that your dal bhat and tea spending is steady. The more you use kharcha, the more accurate and personal its reactions become.

## 💰 Budgets That Make Sense

Budgets in kharcha are built for real Nepali life, not spreadsheets.

## 👥 Friends and Pasal Credit

Money in Nepal often moves between people and shops without cash changing hands on the spot. kharcha handles this natively.

- **Pasal credit** — record what you owe your local shop, and track how it builds up through the month. Pay it off in one go and the balance clears.
- **Friends** — track small loans you gave or received. No more forgetting who owes whom.
- **Settle up** — mark a debt as cleared with a single action.
- **Notes** — add a short note to any credit entry so you remember what it was for.

Everything is private to you. Nobody else sees your balances.
